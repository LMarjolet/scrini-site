Frequently asked questions

Quick answers, each one backed up by the pages that go into detail. If yours isn't here, send it to contact@scrini.fr.

What is Scrini?

An app for tracking the movies, TV shows and anime you watch: check off the episodes you've seen, know where you are in every season, and get a heads-up on upcoming releases. It doesn't stream any content.

Is Scrini free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, no tracking and no selling of your data.

What devices does it work on?

Android 7 and later, iPhone with iOS 15 or later, and any browser at app.scrini.fr — on a computer or a phone, with nothing to install.

Can I use Scrini on a computer?

Yes, at app.scrini.fr: it's the same app, opened in your browser, with the same account and the same library as on your phone. Nothing to install. Release alerts, though, are still a job for the mobile app, which schedules them with your phone's system.

Can I watch movies or TV shows in Scrini?

No. Scrini doesn't host, stream or play any video. It shows which platforms offer each title in the country or region set in the app, and you open the one you subscribe to.

Do I need an account?

Yes: that's what ties your library to you rather than to a device, and gives it back to you when you switch phones. You sign in with a code sent to your email, or with Google or Apple. Signing in with a code only shares your email address, whereas Google and Apple also pass along your name and the address of your profile picture — which the app neither reads nor displays.

What data does Scrini keep?

Your email address, the titles you track along with their list, your rating, your comments and your watch dates, your episode-by-episode progress, your home screen display settings and the app's language. When something breaks, a technical log is kept for thirty days, long enough to figure out what went wrong. Nothing else: no audience measurement, no ad trackers, no location data, no access to your contacts, files or camera. The details are in the privacy policy.

Do notifications go through a third-party service?

No. Release alerts are scheduled locally by the app with your phone's system: no token or identifier is sent to any outside notification service.

Where does the movie and TV show info come from?

From The Movie Database for title pages and air schedules, and from JustWatch via TMDB for streaming availability. These are community-maintained databases: when a network moves a date, it can take a day or two to be corrected there.

Can I get my list back if I leave?

Yes, anytime, without having to ask: the spreadsheet export is in the app, under “Export my data” on your Profile.

How do I delete my account?

From the app in three taps, or from the account deletion page. Deletion is permanent and immediate, and takes all of the account's data with it.

Is anime handled the same way as TV shows?

No, and that's on purpose: anime has its own filter, in search as well as in your lists, because mixing it in makes a library impossible to read. The filter combines with genre, minimum rating and decades. More on that here.

Who publishes Scrini?

Louis Marjolet, the data controller under the GDPR. Contact: contact@scrini.fr.
