Delete your account

App: Scrini

From the app

It's the fastest way, and you don't need anyone's help:

  • Open the side menu, then Settings.
  • Scroll down to Delete my account.
  • Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

If you want to keep your library, remember to export it as a spreadsheet first.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, write to us from your account's email address. Your request will be handled within thirty days.

Email us to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account and your email address.
  • Your library: tracked titles, statuses, checked episodes, ratings, comments and lists.
  • Your preferences and your release alerts.
  • Error reports linked to your account.

Everything goes at once: nothing is kept, no copy, no retention period. Deletion can't be undone — a new account starts from scratch.

Questions?

Write to contact@scrini.fr. See also the privacy policy.
